# Pharma-Drug-Price-Disclosure-Navigator-by-Amy-Kabaria
This 2019 proof-of-concept I created shows how an interactive interface can present complex drug pricing laws and decisions for pharmaceutical manufacturers. While based on laws, this proof-of-concept does not constitute legal advice. The 2019 information it presents may be outdated, and using this tool does not establish an attorney-client relationship.

State pharmaceutical price-transparency laws have since expanded significantly in both number and scope. Following early adopters such as Oregon and California, at least 30 states have adopted drug-price reporting requirements. Newer laws require reporting from across the pharmaceutical supply chain (from health plans, wholesalers, PBMs, and more in addition to manufacturers).
