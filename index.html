About this tool. This is a faithful, dependency-free reconstruction of a 2019 proof-of-concept questionnaire built in QnA Markup to help manufacturers assess their obligations under Oregon's Prescription Drug Price Transparency Act (2018 Or. Laws ch. 7) and its implementing rules (OAR 836-200). The questions, branching logic, and statutory text are reproduced as written in the original — including its original contact line, which is now dated. Informational only; not legal advice, and thresholds and rules may have changed since 2019.
